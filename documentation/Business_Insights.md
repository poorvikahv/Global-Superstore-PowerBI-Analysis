# Global Superstore – Business Insights and Recommendations

## 1. Executive Performance

- Total Sales: 12,642,501.91
- Total Profit: 1,467,457.29
- Total Orders: 25,035
- Total Customers: 1,590
- Profit Margin: 11.61%
- Period Analyzed: January 2011 – December 2014

**Insight:**

The Global Superstore dataset generated approximately 12.64 million in sales and 1.47 million in profit during the four-year analysis period. The overall profit margin was approximately 11.61%.

The business demonstrated positive overall profitability, with opportunities to improve profit margins through better discount management and product-level performance monitoring.

## 2. Sales Trends

- Best-performing year: 2014
- Lowest-performing year: 2011
- Highest-sales month: November 2014
- November 2014 Sales: 555,279.03
- Lowest-sales month: February 2011
- February 2011 Sales: 91,152.16

### Year-over-Year Sales Growth

- 2012: 18.50%
- 2013: 27.20%
- 2014: 26.25%

**Insight:**

Sales increased consistently from 2011 to 2014. The strongest annual growth occurred in 2013, with approximately 27.20% growth compared with 2012.

November recorded the highest monthly sales, indicating a potentially important seasonal sales period.

The business demonstrated sustained growth throughout the analysis period.

## 3. Product and Category Performance

### Category Performance

- Highest-sales category: Technology
- Technology Sales: 4,744,557.50
- Highest-profit category: Technology
- Technology Profit: 663,778.73
- Furniture Sales: 4,110,874.19
- Furniture Profit: 285,204.72
- Office Supplies Sales: 3,787,070.09
- Office Supplies Profit: 518,473.83

### Sub-Category Performance

- Highest-sales sub-category: Phones
- Phones Sales: 1,706,824.13
- Highest-profit sub-category: Copiers
- Copiers Profit: 258,567.55
- Lowest-profit sub-category: Tables
- Tables Profit: -64,083.39

**Insight:**

Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three main product categories.

Phones contributed the highest sub-category sales, while Copiers generated the highest sub-category profit.

Tables generated a negative profit of approximately 64,083.39 despite generating substantial sales. This indicates a need to investigate product costs, pricing, and discount strategies within the Tables sub-category.

## 4. Discount and Profitability

- Overall discount and profit correlation: Approximately -0.316.
- Transaction rows with discounts up to 10% generated approximately 2.11 million in profit.
- Transaction rows with discounts above 50% generated approximately -412,581.70 in profit.
- Higher discount ranges showed weaker average transaction-level profitability.

**Insight:**

The analysis indicates a negative relationship between discount levels and profitability.

Transactions with higher discounts generally showed weaker profitability, and the highest discount range generated substantial negative profit.

This suggests that the business should review discount policies and evaluate whether higher discounts are generating sufficient revenue to justify the reduction in profitability.

Correlation does not establish that discounts alone caused the losses.

## 5. Customer Segment Performance

- Highest-sales segment: Consumer
- Consumer Sales: 6,507,949.42

- Corporate Sales: 3,824,697.73
- Corporate Profit: 441,208.33
- Home Office Sales: 2,309,854.68
- Home Office Profit: 277,009.18

**Insight:**

The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of total sales and profit.

Corporate ranked second, followed by Home Office.

All three segments generated positive overall profit margins, with Home Office having a slightly higher segment-level profit margin than Consumer and Corporate.

The business can continue focusing on Consumer customers while exploring opportunities to improve sales and customer engagement across the other segments.

## 7. Shipping and Returns

- Most-used shipping mode: Standard Class
- Standard Class Orders: 15,154
- Standard Class Sales: 7,578,651.93
- Average Delivery Days: 3.97 days
- Returned Order Records: 1,173
- Distinct Returned Orders: 1,172
- Return Rate: Approximately 4.68%

**Insight:**

Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode and accounted for the largest share of sales.

The average time between order date and ship date was approximately 3.97 days.

The dataset contained 1,172 distinct returned orders, representing approximately 4.68% of the 25,035 distinct orders.

The business should investigate return reasons and review shipping operations to identify opportunities for improving customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

Note: Delivery Days measures the time from order placement to shipping, not the time from order placement to customer delivery.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

1. **Improve discount management:** Review high-discount transactions and introduce discount limits or approval rules where profitability is weak.

2. **Investigate loss-making products:** Analyze the Tables sub-category to identify high costs, pricing issues, or excessive discounts.

3. **Focus on profitable categories:** Continue monitoring Technology and Office Supplies because they contribute substantially to total profit.

4. **Improve regional profitability:** Investigate Southeast Asia's low profit margin and review pricing, logistics, and operational costs.

5. **Strengthen customer segment strategies:** Continue engaging Consumer customers while identifying opportunities to increase Corporate and Home Office sales.

6. **Optimize shipping operations:** Monitor shipping duration and evaluate whether alternative shipping modes can improve operational efficiency.

7. **Monitor returned orders:** Investigate return patterns and identify products or regions that may require quality or service improvements.

## 9. Limitations

- The dataset covers historical transactions from 2011 to 2014 and does not represent current business performance.
- Sales values are reported in the dataset's numeric units; the workbook covers multiple markets and does not provide a single universal reporting currency.
- The analysis identifies patterns and relationships but does not establish causation.
- Delivery Days represents order-to-ship duration, not final customer delivery duration.
- Return Rate uses distinct returned orders divided by distinct total orders.
- Product, regional, and customer findings depend on the filters and aggregation methods used in the dashboard.

## 10. Final Summary

The Global Superstore analysis demonstrates how Power BI can transform retail transaction data into actionable business insights.

The business generated approximately 12.64 million in sales and 1.47 million in profit during 2011–2014, achieving an overall profit margin of 11.61%.

Technology was the leading product category in both sales and profit, while Tables was the only loss-making sub-category in the analysis.

The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales and profit, and APAC was the strongest market.

The analysis also identified lower profitability in Southeast Asia and a negative relationship between discount levels and profit.

These findings highlight the importance of effective discount management, product profitability monitoring, regional performance evaluation, and continuous operational improvement.
